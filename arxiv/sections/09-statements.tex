\subsection{Ethics Declaration}

This study did not involve human participants, personal data, animals, or clinical intervention. It used market, weather, configured tenant, and simulated/read-model evidence. The main ethical and safety concern is misrepresentation of a recommendation preview as an authorized trading or hardware-control system. The implementation prevents this by retaining human review, explicit source status, guarded abstention, and \code{market_execution_enabled=false}.
