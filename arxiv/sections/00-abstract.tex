\begin{abstract}
Battery energy storage system (BESS) arbitrage is often approached as an electricity-price forecasting problem, although forecast error is only indirectly related to the economic quality of a charge-discharge schedule. A prediction can have an acceptable average error and still misorder the few hours that determine arbitrage value, violate state-of-charge timing, or induce excessive throughput. This retrospective study presents an evidence-oriented pipeline for evaluating hourly BESS recommendations for Ukraine's day-ahead market (DAM). Forecast and heuristic candidates are converted into feasible schedules and scored in a common linear-programming (LP) and oracle contour. Regret is the value lost relative to the schedule optimized with realized prices. The main descriptive window contains 18 distinct dates crossed with five configured BESS profiles, yielding 90 paired profile-date rows that share one DAM price process. It is also the latest of four adjacent rolling windows, not an additional independent test.

Raw and horizon-calibrated NBEATSx are reported as co-primary sensitivity sources because their relative status was assessed on the same retrospective window. With calibrated NBEATSx, Schedule/Value Learner V2+ had mean regret 174.77 UAH, versus 310.58 UAH for the strict comparator and 206.37 UAH for V2; the raw-source V2+ mean was 193.36 UAH, versus 310.58 and 225.44 UAH. The V2+ mean was below both comparators in four adjacent rolling windows for both sources. Capacity-normalized regret preserves the ordering, and paired date-cluster bootstrap conclusions are unchanged for block lengths 2, 3, 4, and 6. These are descriptive estimates, not confirmatory holdout results. A lineage audit found that a later artifact historically named \code{dt_v2_plus} is a random forest trained on exact timestamp-shifted copies of the evaluation packet; all four of its nonfallback profile-row changes occur on one delivery date. A post-defense suite of 14 time-separated protocol rows has zero candidate-content overlap throughout and no RF improvement over V2+: latest windows fully abstain, while three earlier-window protocols increase mean regret by 65.18--123.08 UAH at primary seed 42 and remain harmful across three seeds. Separate Hugging Face transformer results use the mirrored packet or report read-model readiness without realized regret. They are retained as engineering diagnostics, not promoted model-performance claims.

The contribution is architectural and evidentiary: a reproducible separation of forecast quality, schedule value, shadow-model evidence, source governance, and execution readiness. Published market rows are the price source for published targets, while forecasts are scenario inputs for unpublished horizons. No new forecasting, LP, random-forest, Decision Transformer, or selective-prediction method is claimed. Intraday-market (IDM) support is an hourly read-model capability, not evaluated bidding performance.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords---}\ battery energy storage system; energy arbitrage; day-ahead market; decision-focused evaluation; regret; rolling-origin evaluation; evidence governance.
