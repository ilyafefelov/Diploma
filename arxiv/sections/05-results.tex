Within this slice, V2+ mean regret is 43.73\% below strict, 15.31\% below V2, and 71.91\% below the raw schedule. These are descriptive paired contrasts. The zero counts mean 0 of 90 profile-date schedules violated the LP's modeled SOC/power checks for each row in Table~\ref{tab:05:results:1}. They do not test thermal behavior, telemetry errors, nonlinear ageing, market rules, or hardware control.

The raw result illustrates the paper's methodological point. NBEATSx is a legitimate forecasting architecture \cite{olivares2023nbeatsx}, but its output is not automatically a good storage schedule. Schedule value depends on extrema order, spread shape, SOC path, and throughput. V2+ operates at the schedule-family level, where these consequences become visible.

The strict comparator remains useful despite its higher regret. It provides a leakage-resistant control and a fallback when learned evidence is weak. V2+ should be interpreted as an improvement over that control within the evaluated packet, not as evidence that the control can be removed from the system.

\subsection{Regret decomposition and residual opportunity}

The regret-decomposition packet explains where V2 still loses value. For the calibrated source, 34 anchors are labeled \code{selector_chose_wrong_family}, with mean V2 regret 355.01 UAH and mean best-candidate regret 219.20 UAH. Nineteen anchors are labeled \code{forecast_extrema_rank_wrong}; 22 are \code{candidate_library_missing_good_schedule}; 11 are \code{strict_already_near_oracle}; and four retain a \code{decision_value_gap_remaining} label.

These categories do not claim causal identification, but they guide model design. ``Selector chose wrong family'' suggests that a better candidate existed in the finite library and that prior-only selection is the bottleneck. ``Candidate library missing good schedule'' indicates that no selector can recover value without new schedules. ``Forecast extrema rank wrong'' suggests a forecast-shape limitation. ``Strict already near oracle'' identifies anchors where aggressive switching has little upside.

An oracle-gap audit performed after the tie-break correction gives a complementary view. For the calibrated source, V2+ selected the best available candidate on 71 of 90 anchors; on 19 anchors a better candidate existed but was not selected. The mean missed-candidate gap on those diagnostic rows is 101.83 UAH. For the raw source, the corresponding counts are 59 and 31, with a mean missed gap of 93.25 UAH. Realized regret is used only to label this audit after selection. The audit therefore motivates a future safe-switch model but cannot serve as an input to the historical decision.

\subsection{Model-lineage audit and non-independent diagnostics}

The next experiment targets rare cases in which a non-V2+ candidate appears beneficial. Its artifact lineage uses \code{dt_v2_plus}, but the command specifies \code{--model-kind random_forest}, \code{--feature-set expanded_prior_context_v1}, 500 trees, maximum depth 6, and a 20 UAH switch threshold. The estimator is a regret-aware random forest, not a Decision Transformer. Its 360 training candidate rows are exact timestamp-shifted copies of its 360 evaluation candidate rows across every audited model input and target. It is therefore an in-packet construction diagnostic, not an out-of-sample experiment.

Across three stored seed runs, mean regret on the same packet is 168.1566 UAH, versus 174.77 UAH for calibrated-source V2+. Median regret is 61.71 versus 67.30 UAH. The selector switches on 4 of 90 profile-date rows and abstains on 86. All four changed profile rows occur on delivery date 2026-04-15; they are not four distinct market episodes. Realized scoring records three wins and one tie, with zero observed 150-UAH tail losses. These correlated, post-hoc counts do not estimate a switching success rate or zero risk.

\subsection{Post-defense temporal replay of the random-forest switch}

Evaluation window 1 trains on windows 4/3/2, window 2 on 4/3, and window 3 on 4. Every evaluation contains 360 candidate rows, 90 profile-date outcomes, and 18 market dates. Exact fingerprints over model inputs and targets find zero train/evaluation overlaps in all 14 materialized protocol rows, rather than the 360 of 360 overlap in the historical packet. This is a post-defense corrective suite, not part of the defended thesis.

\subsection{Separate transformer and read-model lineages}

The Hugging Face Decision Transformer line is separate from the random forest. Its candidate-index smoke uses copies of the same 90 comparison rows shifted backward to exercise sequence construction and training. It selects no V2+ rows and yields mean regret 460.30 UAH, versus 174.77 UAH for calibrated-source V2+. The best-available after-the-fact diagnostic is 153.27 UAH.

This result shows only that this mirrored-row implementation and candidate-index objective did not recover V2+ on the packet. It is not an out-of-sample comparison and does not support a general negative conclusion about Decision Transformer or sequence modeling. The construction is especially narrow given known dependence on trajectory coverage and structure \cite{bhargava2023whendt}. The later random forest learns a value gap and preserves V2+ as fallback; that change must not be attributed to Decision Transformer.

A later HF value-aligned shadow uses a genuine \code{DecisionTransformerModel} backbone over a finite schedule library and deterministic gates. Actions, rewards, and returns-to-go are zero in its scorer input, so it is a transformer-backbone candidate scorer rather than a full return-conditioned DT policy. Its 158.71 UAH frozen diagnostic uses the same mirrored packet, and the operating threshold is selected on that packet. A separate DAM read-model packet covers 32 source-backed days and selects nonfallback candidates on 20, reporting mean selected value 1174.29 UAH but no realized regret. Its reported zero safety failures means zero violations under configured deterministic checks; it does not cover unmodeled thermal, telemetry, ageing, or market constraints.

These results are not directly interchangeable with the 90-row retrospective comparison. The HF proof has a different construction, finite templates, and a day-level packet. Its 158.71 UAH value is a frozen diagnostic. The value-aligned path is an engineering demonstration of guarded selection and abstention.

\subsection{Time-separated Decision Transformer correction}

After the lineage defect was identified, a separate temporal suite trained an actual Hugging Face \code{DecisionTransformerModel} on earlier rolling windows and evaluated later windows. Its trajectories contain nonzero action targets, regret-derived rewards, and nonzero returns-to-go; the train/evaluation candidate-content overlap count is zero in every run. The suite crosses two source lanes, three temporal protocols, candidate-index cross entropy and a decision-aware regret/value objective, and three seeds, for 36 runs.

No run improves on frozen V2+. The decision-aware objective abstains to V2+ and ties in 18/18 runs. Candidate-index cross entropy ties in 15/18 and is harmful in 3/18, increasing mean regret by 10.12, 12.28, and 21.09 UAH in the affected protocol/seed runs. A 10-epoch sensitivity converts those switches to abstention rather than gains. This is stronger evidence than the mirrored smoke because it tests genuine temporal separation, but it is still retrospective and data-limited. It is published as negative DT evidence rather than hidden or promoted.

\subsection{Preregistered differentiable forecast-to-storage suite}

Version 1.2 also preregistered a distinct differentiable experiment before training. MLP and transformer price correctors were trained either with forecast loss or with realized storage value differentiated through strictly convex \code{cvxpylayers} problems. The decision loss uses each tenant's capacity, power, starting SOC, efficiency, SOC bounds, and degradation contract; checkpoints use only an earlier inner-validation slice. Every schedule was then recomputed and scored by the frozen strict LP/oracle contour. The experiment uses the same two sources, three temporal protocols, and three seeds, yielding 72 runs of 90 profile rows over 18 dates. All safety-violation and content-overlap counts are zero; all 36 decision-focused runs use the differentiable layer without surrogate fallback.

\subsection{Negative and near-miss evidence}

Additional experiments show where the repository retained its fallback. The best TFT quantile V2+ row has mean regret 225.47 UAH and median 121.00 UAH, worse than calibrated-source V2+ on the same packet. TFT remains useful for uncertainty and complementary schedules \cite{lim2021tft,jiang2024ptft}, but this packet does not support replacement.

A richer Poland lag-24 route has mean regret 177.34 UAH and median 39.46 UAH. A prior-only Poland veto has mean 167.05 UAH and median 55.97 UAH, with 34 challenger rows and 56 fallback rows. Its stored improvement ratio is 4.41\%, below the internal 5\% screen. These are retrospective near-miss diagnostics, not independent selection evidence.

The V13 source-readiness gate also remains blocked. Staged safe-switch support reaches the required 20 prior examples for the configured tenant/source pairs, but explicit OREE DAM/IDM source-publication evidence for the relevant preview contour is incomplete. A July 2026 public probe retrieved 24 DAM rows, but the rows had no source publication timestamp; HTTP \code{Date} and first-observed time were retained as retrieval evidence only. The official XMtrade/PXS specification identifies an authenticated, signed response path, but credentials or a valid signed export are required. \code{promotable_v13_permitted_training_rows=0} remains the correct state.
