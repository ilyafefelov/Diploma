 Data Portal 

 Data Portal is a web platform provided by Nord Pool, where users can access detailed electricity market data. It serves as a centralized hub for viewing, analyzing, and downloading information related to power trading across the Nord Pool market and serviced areas.

 Interested in purchasing? Have questions? Send us an inquiry!

 Inquire now 

 Nord Pool Data Portal is organized into various sections, covering a wide range of market and system data, offering a rich resource for analysis.

 Auctions – Includes Day-Ahead, Intraday Auctions and EPAD Auctions.

 UK Auctions – Includes N2EX Day-Ahead Auction, SEB-GB and GB Half Hour Auction.

 Intraday – Provides insights into continuous intraday trading.

 Power System Data – Gathers information from Balancing Markets (mFRR), Production, Consumption, and Hydro Reservoir Reserves.

 Reports - allows users to easily create customized reports and export them as CSV or Excel files. Historical datasets are available dating back to 1992.

 Invoicing & Redistribution Policy 

 Nord Pool Data Services users pay a pre-determined access fee “Data Fee” charged annually to access to a given data feed. All fees are charged per calendar year and excluding VAT.

 Each customer will be entitled to one (1) API account included with their Nord Pool API subscriptions, regardless of the number of subscriptions held. This account will provide access to all subscriptions purchased by the customer, thereby simplifying access management and reducing the need for multiple API credentials. Additional API accounts may be purchased separately if required.

 A redistribution fee is applied in addition to the data fee, where applicable, in accordance with Euronext Group Data Product Agreement – Master Terms and the relevant Product Terms for each Nord Pool market data redistribution use case.

 Special terms apply to trading members, students, researchers, and members of academic entities.
