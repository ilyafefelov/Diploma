```text
Do a full project review like an academic committee.
```

```text
Review the market rules, Dagster pipelines, experiments, thesis, dashboard, and give ADR/PRD/data flow/fix plan.
```

```text
Run the full Diploma thesis audit again and compare it to competitors and academic sources.
```

## Thesis-safe wording

```text
The system is a DAM delivery-day operator recommendation preview and offline read-model evidence system. V2+ is the current headline offline research challenger under a frozen strict LP/oracle comparator. DT, LAVA, TFT, and Poland-context experiments are research or shadow lanes and do not enable market execution.
```

## Unsafe wording

```text
The system submits bids.
The Decision Transformer controls the battery.
LAVA is promoted.
Poland features replace Ukrainian training data.
The project is full deployed DFL.
```
