# Project Intro Video Design

## Style Prompt

Commission-facing technical product explainer for a Ukrainian BESS operator-preview repository. The tone is precise, evidence-backed, and calm: academic engineering proof, not a startup promo.

## Colors

- Background: `#08111f` deep ink
- Panel: `#102a43` steel blue
- Secondary panel: `#173f5f` muted cyan-blue
- Primary accent: `#b7ff3c` energy lime
- Evidence accent: `#7dd3fc` source cyan
- Boundary accent: `#fbbf24` review amber
- Foreground: `#f7fbff` near-white
- Muted text: `#b8c7d9`

## Typography

- Display voice: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif
- Data and UI voice: Consolas, Cascadia Mono, monospace
- Body voice: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif

## Motion

- Medium-energy explainer pacing.
- Push/slide and soft crossfade transitions.
- Entrance animations only inside scenes; final scene may fade to black.
- Data marks drift or pulse slowly to keep the composition alive.

## What Not To Do

- Do not present the system as a trading bot.
- Do not claim live market execution, deployed DT control, or market-submittable bids.
- Do not use synthetic price fallback language.
- Do not let generated visuals alter exact metrics, labels, or boundary text.
