# Current arXiv guidance and submission checklist

Checked 2026-07-12 against official arXiv help and policy pages.

## Positioning

- Primary category: `eess.SY`.
- Do not request `cs.LG` cross-list for version 1. The official ML
  classification guide recommends the application domain when that community is
  the main audience, and the current learned-selector evaluation is not OOS.
- Comment: `Based on a Master's thesis defended in 2026.`
- arXiv is a moderated preprint repository, not peer review. The manuscript must
  still be topical, refereeable, self-contained, and responsibly presented.

## Operational checklist

1. Upload TeX sources, not a TeX-generated PDF alone.
2. Keep ancillary evidence under a top-level `anc/` directory.
3. Verify the exact server-generated PDF, references, figures, metadata, and
   source manifest before final submission.
4. Use ASCII metadata; keep the abstract within the submission form limit.
5. Publish a new immutable repository tag/release and cite its direct URL.
6. Choose the license deliberately; arXiv states the choice is irrevocable.
7. Disclose AI assistance accurately and retain sole-author responsibility.
8. Expect possible endorsement. Since the 2026 policy update, institutional
   email alone is not enough for automatic endorsement; the visible account has
   a Gmail address and no listed submissions, so personal endorsement may be
   required after selecting `eess.SY` in a draft.
