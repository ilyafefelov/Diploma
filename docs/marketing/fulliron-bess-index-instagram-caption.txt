A small public path project from my energy-arbitrage work:

Ukraine BESS Arbitrage Index.

The public page asks a simple question:
How much value could a standard battery capture on Ukrainian day-ahead electricity prices?

It uses official OREE DAM price data, computes a source-backed BESS dispatch receipt, and keeps the claim boundary explicit:

No bids.
No market execution.
No utility integration claim.
Public demo only.

This is part of my post-defense work around energy analytics, optimization, forecasting, ML boundaries, and product design.
