# Erratum: model lineage and safe-switch evidence

Date: 2026-07-12

Applies to the Master's thesis defended in 2026 and its preserved final PDF.
The defended PDF remains an archival record and is not silently replaced. This
erratum corrects the scientific interpretation of the artifact historically
named `dt_v2_plus`.

## Correction

The canonical 168.1566 UAH safe-switch result is **not a Decision
Transformer**. It was generated by a `RandomForestRegressor` with 500 trees,
maximum depth 6, and minimum leaf size 1. The name `dt_v2_plus` is only a
historical artifact identifier inherited from the research lane.

The more complex transformer architecture exists in a separate lineage. The HF
scorer loads Hugging Face `DecisionTransformerModel` with hidden size 64, two
layers, two attention heads, and 170,400 parameters. In the frozen scorer path,
actions, rewards, and returns-to-go are zeroed, so it is accurately described as
a `DecisionTransformerModel`-backbone candidate scorer rather than a full
return-conditioned Decision Transformer policy.

## Evidence limitation discovered after defense

The RF's 360 training candidate rows are exact timestamp-shifted copies of its
360 evaluation candidate rows. Every compared model input and target is equal;
only identifiers, split fields, and timestamps differ. All four nonfallback
profile-row switches occur on one delivery date, 2026-04-15, across profiles
sharing one DAM price path. Therefore:

- 168.1566 UAH is an exact-mirror in-packet pipeline diagnostic, not an
  out-of-sample or final-holdout estimate;
- 4/90 switches are four profile outcomes on one market episode, not four
  independent dates;
- 0/4 observed tail losses is not an estimate of zero risk;
- identical results across three nominal seeds and the stored p-value have no
  valid inferential interpretation.

The HF frozen 158.7121 UAH diagnostic also uses the mirrored packet and selects
its threshold on that packet. A separate 32-day read-model audit reports 20/32
nonfallback days and mean selected value 1174.29 UAH, but does not contain a
realized-regret outcome. These results must not be combined as independent
evidence.

## Corrected labels

Every thesis occurrence of "corrected DT/V2+ safe-switch", "DT safe-switch",
or a transformer attribution for 168.16 UAH should be read as:

> random-forest V2+ safe-switch diagnostic (historical artifact identifier
> `dt_v2_plus`; exact-mirror, not out-of-sample)

Figures that label the 168.16 UAH bar as DT/V2+ should use the same corrected
label. Statements that both the RF and HF follow-ups are transformer-based are
incorrect: only the separate HF lineage uses a transformer backbone.

## Unchanged main conclusion

The thesis headline remains Schedule/Value Learner V2+: 174.77 UAH calibrated-
source mean regret, compared with 310.58 UAH for the strict comparator and
206.37 UAH for V2, with descriptive robustness across four adjacent replay
windows. This remains retrospective evidence over 18 distinct dates and five
configured BESS profiles, not confirmatory prospective validation or market
execution.
